Garmin workout files

Structured Garmin Connect workouts, generated a couple weeks ahead at a time. Download individually or grab a whole week as a .zip.
Workout files generated by scripts/build_garmin_workouts.py, indexed by scripts/build_workouts_manifest.py into data/workouts/index.json. Import a file at connect.garmin.com/modern/workouts, then sync it to your watch from the Garmin Connect app.
